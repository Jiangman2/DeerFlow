New placement/resources
Exact per-node Deployments avoid a spread plan overcommitting a single node.

parser timeouts, queue settings and other environment are preserved.

discovers only its own headless Service. Labels/network policies and quotas
are captured for review before any real deployment. Server-side Deployment
dry-run does NOT prove Pod admission, scheduling or runtime readiness.
