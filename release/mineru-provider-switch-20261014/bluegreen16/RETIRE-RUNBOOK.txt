This tool makes at most TWO live Kubernetes API patches, both in deer-flow:
  1. deer-flow-mineru-dispatcher: spec.replicas 1 -> 0

WeKnora's entire persistent queue to empty. New production traffic is allowed
to remain active in the unchanged six-slot new pool.
