## Behavior

- Append through the AIO file API's native `append=True` operation instead of
  a client-side read/concatenate/write sequence. This prevents a failed pre-read
  from turning an append into an overwrite and avoids lost updates.
- For directory-inclusive AIO glob results, scan one eligible match past the
  requested cap before declaring truncation. Exactly-full results are complete,
  while a dropped eligible match still reports truncation.
- A zero-result cap returns no matches and reports truncation when an eligible
  match exists instead of leaking one result past the caller's limit.

## Deliberately deferred

Remote `list_dir`, glob, and grep failure/truncation propagation spans AIO,
E2B, BoxLite, OpenSandbox, and Tenki and introduces shared command/parser
contracts. It remains a separate batch so those providers can be adapted and
tested together rather than partially changing their public behavior here.
